## Sustained and recovery qualification

Current traffic/resource targets describe the planned management/indexed-query fixture, not a validated Storefront workload. After the first substantive Storefront contract, add its actual published-search/recommendation mix, trusted-context matrix and owning latency/privacy gates to existing profile plumbing before claiming cross-API readiness. Do not certify Storefront from Admin-only probes.

Use existing `make capacity TARGET=api PROFILE=endpoint-split MODE=<diagnostic|alpha|production>`, `TARGET=namespace PROFILE=watch`, `TARGET=category PROFILE=hierarchy`. Extend their runners/verifiers and existing mode/preflight plumbing; do not invent feature-specific public targets. Native-push/reconciliation traffic retains its existing owning profiles and stricter gates. Query probes and provider-outage plumbing are implementation work, not currently available flags.

Verify the retained >=5M-product/10K-category fixture, actual two APIs/two controllers/one Git, provider/index coverage and the plan's declared CPU/memory/disk/payload/embedding configuration. Offer 100 structured+25 fresh ranked-window reads/s distributed across 16 authorized test principals, 2,500 watches and 20 admitted changes/s for the full 30 minutes alongside separately measured authoring/native-push traffic. Continuation/backward probes are additional and separately attributed. At 600-second TTL expect <=15,000 steady-state fresh windows under the 20,000 global/2,048 principal-scope limits. Meet spec latency/error gates and no OOM/resource drift, unexpected restarts, missing admitted changes, invalid ranking or authorization leaks. If a mode is not implemented, a lower-capability diagnostic run cannot certify the rich-query workload. Record offered, completed, throttled, timed-out and failed work separately.

Five minutes at twice offered read/change rates must retain correctness, explicit overload and recover <=30 s after load removal. Reuse owned `make chaos CHAOS_PROFILE=api-restart`, `controller-restart`, `journal-interruption`; test a bounded provider outage through extended existing harness. Recovery <=60 s after readiness or stricter existing deadline. Preserve unrelated deployment/storage/checkpoints and record sole fixture ownership before faults. No broad cleanup/reseed.

Receipts identify exact commit/config/index/model versions, full durations, actual topology/resources, traffic mix, latency distributions and domain oracles. Diagnostic instrumentation/short tests do not prove production qualification. Normal `make generate TARGET=all`, `make test`, `make check TARGET=all` and full `make pr-ready` remain required; capacity does not replace them. Update docs/contributor schemas as implemented, not as promised runtime support. At closure audit ADR applicability/status/index and spec completion explicitly.
